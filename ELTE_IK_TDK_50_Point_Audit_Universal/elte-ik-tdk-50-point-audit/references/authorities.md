# Authorities, precedence, and scope

Checked 2026-10-05. Re-check live sources when auditing a later call.

## Precedence

Apply the newest applicable official source. Use the live registration system
and destination call over this reference if they conflict. Record the exact
conflict and date rather than silently selecting a rule.

1. Current destination call and supplement.
2. Current ELTE IK institutional TDK call and Hungarian/English companion page.
3. Current ELTE IK AI, ethics, and programme rules.
4. Older calls only for historical context, never to reintroduce superseded
   requirements.

## Current written-paper allocation

The 2026 ELTE IK IT Science Section page allocates 50 paper points:

- novelty and quality of development: 8;
- topic and content: 8;
- verification of results: 8;
- literature review: 6;
- formal presentation and style: 6;
- scientific value: 6;
- summary opinion and overall quality: 8.

The institutional conference evaluates both the paper and oral presentation.
This skill audits only the written-paper 50 points.

## Current OTDK written-review process

The 38th OTDK Information and Communication Technology Section uses two written
reviewers, each up to 50 points, and the written-paper score is their mean. If
their totals differ by strictly more than 15 points, reconciliation is required
and a third reviewer is used if the difference persists. Do not invent a
third-review combination formula.

The current call requires an informatics result rather than merely using IT as
a tool. Its current submission components include an abstract, AI declaration,
and a 2000–4000-character student-result statement. The current Hungarian
supplement defines the abstract as 800–2500 characters with four components of
at least 200 characters each: research justification, methods, student's own
results, and main conclusions.

## ELTE IK MSc acceptance note

The current institutional page states that a TDK paper may be accepted as an MSc
thesis only when the TDK and Neptun versions are identical and remain unchanged
until the final examination. It is not accepted as a BSc thesis under that page.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not import older requirements such as a printed copy or a mandatory ACM
category unless the current live call independently requires them. Do not score
the oral presentation, invent award thresholds, or convert 50 points into a
course grade.
