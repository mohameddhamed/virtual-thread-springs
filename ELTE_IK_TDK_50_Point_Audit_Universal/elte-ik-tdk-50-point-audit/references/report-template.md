# Audit report template

## 1. Executive result

- Audit scope and governing call
- Paper revision and evidence cutoff
- Research mode(s)
- Result: `X/50` when complete, otherwise `X–Y/50`
- Resolution: `R/50 items resolved`
- Critical blockers
- One-sentence overall opinion

## 2. Evidence inventory

| Artifact | Exact version | Inspected | Limitation |
|---|---|---:|---|

## 3. Critical blockers and human decisions

For each blocker give observed evidence, why it matters, and the exact human or
artifact check required. Keep blocker severity separate from points.

## 5. Fifty-item Boolean ledger

| ID | Status | Point | Evidence | Finding or correction |
|---|---|---:|---|---|

Include all IDs N01–N08, T01–T08, V01–V08, L01–L06, F01–F06,
S01–S06, and Q01–Q08.

## 6. Reviewer summary opinion

- strongest evidence-backed qualities;
- most material weaknesses;
- novelty and contribution judgment;
- verification and reproducibility judgment;
- scientific value and publication-potential judgment;
- overall quality, without invented grade or award threshold.

## 7. Score-changing correction plan

| Priority | Recoverable IDs | Required correction or evidence | Owner | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Order by blockers, validity, recoverable points, dependencies, then effort.

## 8. OTDK and submission readiness

Check cover/title metadata, abstract, student-result statement, AI declaration,
student ownership, data/artifact access, PDF/source parity, file-size and
supplement constraints, and version identity where applicable. Keep oral
presentation outside the score.

## 9. Two-reviewer comparison, when applicable

| Reviewer | Final written score | Complete | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|

Report mean only when both are complete. Flag reconciliation/third review only
when the absolute difference is strictly greater than 15.

## 10. Human verification checklist

List claims, proofs, runs, data permissions, authorship boundaries, integrity
risks, and visual defects that require a named human decision.
