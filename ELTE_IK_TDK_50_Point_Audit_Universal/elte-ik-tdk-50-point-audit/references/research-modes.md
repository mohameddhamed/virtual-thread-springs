# Research-mode evidence routing

Select every materially relevant mode. Mixed work must satisfy the relevant
checks of every selected mode.

## Theoretical or formal

Inspect definitions, assumptions, quantifiers, lemmas, proofs, algorithms,
counterexamples, edge cases, formal certificates, and independent proof checks.
Verification may be a correct proof rather than an experiment.

## Empirical ML or data

Inspect lawful data provenance, splits, leakage prevention, preprocessing,
baselines, tuning fairness, seeds, uncertainty, effect sizes, ablations,
robustness, predictions, raw outputs, and reproducible configurations.

## Systems or software

Inspect source revision, builds, tests, architecture rationale, workloads,
instrumentation, raw measurements, fair comparisons, correctness, failure
behavior, performance variance, security, and reproducible environments.

## Human-subject or HCI

Inspect protocol, ethics/consent, sampling, instruments, privacy, anonymization,
construct validity, bias, missing data, analysis, coding reliability, adverse
events, and limits of transfer.

## Simulation or numerical

Inspect the model, solver, parameters, initial and boundary conditions,
calibration, validation, tolerances, stability, convergence, sensitivity,
checksums, logs, and comparison with theory or observations.

## Qualitative or design

Inspect case selection, protocol, artifacts, coding records, triangulation,
researcher influence, reflexivity, saturation or coverage claims, alternative
interpretations, and transfer limits.

## Mixed mode

Do not let strength in one branch hide failure in another. For example, a novel
system with a user study must satisfy both system-verification and human-subject
requirements for claims that rely on each branch.
