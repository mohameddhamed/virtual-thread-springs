# TDK authorities and stage-specific requirements

Read before claiming submission compliance or selecting the presentation budget. This is a dated starting point, not a permanent rulebook. Recheck the call, supplement and event instructions applicable to the student's actual event.

## Source order and scope

1. Current applicable destination call, supplement, official submission form and event/session instructions. A current organizer notice may clarify the actual session or form. Record material conflicts; a form cannot silently waive a governing rule.
2. Current ELTE institutional TDK call for the relevant semester.
3. Applicable ELTE AI/research/ethics policies.
4. Supervisor guidance, templates and existing audit reports.
5. Historical calls and editorial defaults, explicitly labelled.

Faculty TDK, national OTDK and MSc final examination are distinct events. Establish the target event/year and section, inferring them from supplied links/materials when possible. Do not apply all national rules automatically to a faculty submission, or reuse a thesis-defense roster, professional-exam subject list or MSc talk length.

## Checked snapshot: 16 November 2026

**ELTE autumn faculty call:** abstract/application date 16 November 2026; ordinary paper upload 3 December; conference 17 December. The public call gives no exact cutoff hour, abstract typography/length rule, talk duration or assigned jury roster. A lack of detail in this call does not prove no rule exists in the form or a session notice. Use explicit dates in Europe/Budapest; do not repeat relative wording such as "tomorrow", assume 23:59, or describe the deadline gap as a fixed two weeks.

For students seeking same-semester MSc thesis acceptance, the call specifies an earlier simultaneous TDK/Neptun upload by 1 December and identity of the accepted versions; do not change frozen material on their behalf. These administrative requirements are separate from drafting an abstract or preparing a talk.

**38th OTDK abstract:** 800-2,500 characters, with four components of at least 200 characters each: research justification, methods, student's own achieved results, and conclusions. The same body is included in the paper appendix and uploaded to the national system; names/institution/supervisor are separate system metadata. Five keywords absent from the title are requested. The current call does not carry forward the old mandatory ACM-XML submission. Hungarian-native or Hungarian-programme students require Hungarian abstracts/presentations; others may use English. Verify the student's applicable language when this rule applies rather than inferring native language from a name.

**38th OTDK presentation/reviews:** 12-minute presentation followed by 8-minute discussion; written paper and oral presentation each have a separate maximum of 50. Two written reviews are used; their complete scores are averaged. A difference strictly greater than 15 points triggers the specified reconciliation process and, if unresolved, a third reviewer. Reviewers are confidential; do not infer they are jury members. Current national guidance makes the reviews available about a week before the event.

The national abstract excludes results obtained after the qualifying institutional conference. Later results may be mentioned in the national presentation only with an explicit label that they are outside the submitted paper. An early faculty abstract must likewise distinguish achieved evidence from planned work, without inventing results.

## Supervisor resources and audit use

- https://grm.web.elte.hu/ — TDK templates and current guidance.
- https://grm.web.elte.hu/supervision/ELTE_IK_TDK_50_Point_Audit_Universal.zip — distribution containing the written-paper audit skill.

The existing written audit uses seven allocations: 8/8/8/6/6/6/8 for novelty, topic/content, verification, literature, form/style, scientific value and overall quality. Its 50 checks can help identify evidence and prepare answers; they are not the separate oral rubric. Reuse a supplied audit, preserving its conclusions. Neither skill here requires installing/running that audit or obtaining a new score.

## Record and limitations

For material rules record source title/URL or supplied notice identifier, effective/update date when stated, access date, destination/scope, and verified/provisional status. Prefer official companion-language pages or linked PDFs when the first page is inaccessible. A "Loading" shell is not a successful source read.

If a live form or roster is unavailable, request its relevant text only when it changes the result; do not ask for credentials. Continue with labelled editorial defaults where possible. Never claim to have verified a form limit, cutoff, jury assignment or source that was not read. Public organizing-committee names are not an assigned session jury.
