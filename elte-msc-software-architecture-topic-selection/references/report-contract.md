# Report contract

Produce a self-contained, anonymized report. Use the scorer's calculated counts, fractions, tiers and order; add evidence-based synthesis without changing arithmetic.

## 1. Basis and boundaries

Record anonymous project ID/title, assessor, date, curriculum, maturity, artifacts/revisions, missing evidence, map version and currently checked source findings. Distinguish fixed catalog evidence, current individual eligibility and older/provisional syllabus content. State that breadth is not a faculty mark or acceptance probability and the inherited anchor cutoff is advisory.

## 2. Safest three or not-ready verdict

Recommend only above-cutoff candidates passing the availability gate, in deterministic order. Give the decisive anchor, current artifact and any remaining P work for each. If any member's individual availability is unknown, label the set **administratively provisional**. If fewer than three qualify, give a conspicuous not-ready verdict and distinguish relationship failures from availability failures. Never claim certification.

## 3. All-topic ranking and supplemental status

Rank every one of the 19 exact dropdown subjects, retaining original numbers/names. Show tier, C/P/N counts, exact lower/upper fractions, rounded percentage range, C/P anchors, individual eligibility and decisive gap. Insert this bold line between qualifying and failed relationship tiers:

> **SUPERVISOR CERTIFICATION CUTOFF - topics below this line should not be selected without new substantive evidence and supervisor review.**

Keep entry 20 visible in a separate supplemental panel if not verified eligible. Give its full relationship result and its eligibility blocker. If verified, include it in the candidate ranking without renumbering or dropping any of the 19. Show unavailable regular entries even though they cannot be recommended. Relation tiers alone never imply administrative availability.

## 4. Preferred choices

Treat one/two preferences as partial; the final set must have three. Stop for below-cutoff, unavailable or unknown-supplemental choices. Flag unknown regular eligibility as provisional. For a defensible alternative outside the recommended set, compare it with a displaced subject: decisive evidence, actual thesis centrality, count/breadth tradeoffs and remaining completion work. Either provide that comparison from inspected evidence or explicitly leave it unresolved; do not merely say alternatives are allowed.

## 5. Complete atomic audit

For every subject, including Appendix A, account for every atom exactly once:

- C: atom label, precise current pointer and thesis-specific justification;
- P: label, present scope/design basis, justification, smallest work, acceptance evidence and feasibility;
- N: label and honest reason for non-link.

Show denominator, counts, fractions, tier, anchors, eligibility source and minimum natural work for each. Do not omit failed subjects or unsupported atoms. Do not suppress the older Advanced Java scope caveat or historical Scalable enterprise technology examples.

## 6. Next actions and reruns

Prioritize existing evidence with weak pointers, natural planned work for decisive anchors and outstanding individual eligibility confirmation. Identify which suggestions should not be pursued because they distort scope. Name the material-change and pre-approval rerun triggers and respect the administrative deadline.

Close with the full-subject study obligation: choosing a thesis-related subject does not reduce the final-exam scope to linked atoms. The student and appropriate faculty/supervisor decide; this audit neither grades the thesis nor certifies or submits a selection.
