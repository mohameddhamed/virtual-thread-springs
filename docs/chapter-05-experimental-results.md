# Chapter 5: Experimental Results

## 5.0 Empirical Reporting Principles and Planned Visualizations

In accordance with empirical software engineering standards, this chapter adheres strictly to **pure empirical reporting**. It documents directly measured observations, computed descriptive distributions, event logs, and cryptographic validations without speculative interpretation or causal overreach. Theoretical synthesis, architectural explanations, and answers to the research questions are formally deferred to Chapter 6.

To ensure transparency and prevent narrative bias, all performance metrics in this chapter adhere to the **Dual-Window Reporting Mandate** established in `CLM-METH-02`. Every summary table reports both:
1.  **Whole-Run Metrics ($t \in [0\text{s}, 55\text{s}]$):** Capturing full benchmark execution including client ramp-up and ramp-down.
2.  **Steady-State Hold-Window Metrics ($t \in [15\text{s}, 45\text{s}]$):** Filtering strictly to the 30-second constant-concurrency equilibrium stage.

All metrics are derived directly from the verified, reproducible dataset [`docs/evidence-freeze/metric-recalculations.csv`](file:///Users/mohamedhamed/Documents/virtual-thread-springs/docs/evidence-freeze/metric-recalculations.csv) generated by [`scripts/recalculate_metrics.py`](file:///Users/mohamedhamed/Documents/virtual-thread-springs/scripts/recalculate_metrics.py).
