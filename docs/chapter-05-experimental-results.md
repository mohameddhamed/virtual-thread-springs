# Chapter 5: Experimental Results

## 5.0 Empirical Reporting Principles and Planned Visualizations

In accordance with empirical software engineering standards, this chapter adheres strictly to **pure empirical reporting**. It documents directly measured observations, computed descriptive distributions, event logs, telemetry streams, and cryptographic validations without speculative interpretation or causal overreach. Architectural explanations, queueing theory modeling, and formal answers to the research questions are reserved for Chapter 6.

To ensure transparency and prevent narrative bias, all performance metrics in this chapter adhere to the **Dual-Window Reporting Mandate** established in `CLM-METH-02`. Every summary table reports both:
1.  **Whole-Run Metrics ($t \in [0\text{s}, 55\text{s}]$):** Capturing full benchmark execution including client ramp-up and ramp-down.
2.  **Steady-State Hold-Window Metrics ($t \in [15\text{s}, 45\text{s}]$):** Filtering to the selected 30-second constant-concurrency hold interval; this completion-time window is not a proof of equilibrium.

All metrics are derived directly from the verified, reproducible dataset [`docs/evidence-freeze/metric-recalculations.csv`](../docs/evidence-freeze/metric-recalculations.csv) generated by [`scripts/recalculate_metrics.py`](../scripts/recalculate_metrics.py), and cross-referenced with [`docs/evidence-freeze/run-family-matrix.csv`](../docs/evidence-freeze/run-family-matrix.csv) and [`docs/evidence-freeze/claim-evidence-ledger.md`](../docs/evidence-freeze/claim-evidence-ledger.md).

### 5.3.1 Key Primary Matrix Empirical Observations
1.  **Monitor Pinning Event Invariant (`CLM-RQ1-01`):** Every trial under `payments-synchronized` recorded exactly **108** `jdk.VirtualThreadPinned` events in Stratum 1 (and 110 events in Stratum 5). In every synchronized execution, the pinning count matched the total completed HTTP requests exactly 1:1, showing that each tested synchronized trial recorded 108 qualifying pin events for 108 completed requests; the thresholded events do not prove that every entering virtual thread pinned.
2.  **JFR Event Suppression via `ReentrantLock` (`CLM-RQ1-03`):** Every trial under `payments-reentrant-lock` recorded exactly **0** pinning events across all JFR event streams.
3.  **Throughput Invariance Across Mutex Types (`CLM-RQ1-04`):** Despite the total elimination of JFR pinning events under `ReentrantLock`, the observed hold-window throughput was descriptively similar in these three process repetitions: **$1.967\text{ req/s}$** for `synchronized` versus **$1.956\text{ req/s}$** for `reentrant-lock` (a minor difference of $0.011\text{ req/s}$ or $0.56\%$).
4.  **The Ramp-Up Latency Inflection in Platform Threads (`CLM-METH-02`):** In `platform-synchronized`, the whole-run median latency was recorded as **$3917.56\text{ ms}$**, whereas the steady-state hold-window median latency was **$4929.49\text{ ms}$**. Including the 15-second ramp-up stage depressed reported median latency by **$1011.93\text{ ms}$ ($-25.83\%$)**.
5.  **Diagnostic Ceiling (`no-lock`):** Removing mutual exclusion increased steady-state throughput from $1.967\text{ req/s}$ to **$16.489\text{ req/s}$** ($+738.3\%$) and reduced median response latency from $4966.79\text{ ms}$ to **$506.61\text{ ms}$** ($-89.8\%$).

### 5.4.1 Dispersion Findings and Methodological Boundaries
*   **The AQS Barging Dispersion Signature (`CLM-RQ1-05`):** While `synchronized` exhibited an extremely narrow trial-to-trial median latency range of **$\Delta = 1.28\text{ ms}$** ($\sigma = 0.70\text{ ms}$) in Stratum 1 and $\Delta = 7.24\text{ ms}$ ($\sigma = 3.63\text{ ms}$) in Stratum 5, `reentrant-lock` exhibited a range of **$\Delta = 29.78\text{ ms}$** ($\sigma = 16.53\text{ ms}$) in Stratum 1 and **$\Delta = 32.18\text{ ms}$** ($\sigma = 18.07\text{ ms}$) in Stratum 5. This wider dispersion is consistent with the scheduling behavior permitted by non-fair AQS direct-CAS barging, but acquisition order and causality were not directly measured.
*   **Descriptive Framing Mandate (`CLM-METH-03`):** Because the sample size per condition is $N=3$, these dispersion metrics are reported purely as descriptive mechanism validations. No inferential claims (e.g., ANOVA, Student's t-tests, or confidence intervals) are asserted.

### 5.6.1 Key JNI Empirical Observations
1.  **Silent Carrier Captivity (`CLM-RQ2-01`):** In all 9 trials, steady-state throughput collapsed to **$1.967\text{ req/s}$** and steady-state $p50$ latency rose to **$4963.36\text{ ms}$**, proving that foreign blocking system calls hold carrier threads captive under single-carrier scheduling (`parallelism=1`).
2.  **Zero-Event JFR Blind Spot (`CLM-RQ2-01`):** Across all 9 trials (and all 27 Stratum 3 trials), JFR recorded exactly **0** `jdk.VirtualThreadPinned` events. Because native calls execute directly on the OS thread without invoking `VirtualThread.park()`, no unmount failure is detected by the JVM runtime.
3.  **Low Steady-State CPU Utilization (`CLM-RQ2-02`):** Companion process telemetry recorded steady-state CPU utilization of **$1.04\% \pm 0.12\%$** (peak steady-state $\le 8.5\%$). This empirically disproves the 100% CPU assertion from earlier exploratory notes (`DISC-02`), proving that carrier threads remain in kernel sleep rather than active spin-loops.
